1Approvals and audit log

Both pages live in the Lanekeeper shell. Approvals: an Admin queue with Pending, Stale, Expired and Decided tabs, a detail drawer with consequence first, and live updates. Audit: a filterable, hash-chained log with attribution source and confidence, a detail drawer with evidence, and an integrity panel. Cursor-made changes are solid dark chips and unknown authors are dashed italic chips, so both stand out and filter in one click. Rows and filters in each frame are clickable.
