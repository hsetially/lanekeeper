Role-based navigation · Viewer
1lRecommended · destinations hidden, actions disabled with a reason
1mAlternative · destinations disabled with a lock and tooltip
1nDecision

Hide destinations. Disable actions and say why.

A navigation item is a place a role can never use, so it is removed. An action lives on a page the role can already see, so it stays in place, disabled, with the role it needs and who can grant it.
